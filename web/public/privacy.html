Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

This app shows the British Transplant Games schedule and lets you keep track of which events your family or team are taking part in. This page covers both the iOS and Android apps.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • No data is ever sent to us, or to any third party. There is no server for it to be sent to — the app just reads the public schedule and stores anything personal to you on your own device.

The schedule itself

The app downloads the published Games schedule (events, venues, times) over the internet so it can show it to you. This is public information about the event, not about you, and the request for it doesn't carry any personal information.

Your plan — people, bookings and notes

If you use the app to add people (for example, family members) and allocate them to events, that information — names, which events they're booked onto, and any notes you add — is stored only on your own device.

  • Android: this data stays on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere, and we have no access to it.
  • iOS: this data syncs across your own devices using Apple's iCloud, under your own Apple ID. It is not sent to us or to anyone else — Apple provides that sync, not us, and we have no access to it either.

This applies equally if the people you add are children — their names are just a label stored locally on your device (or synced within your own iCloud account), the same as any other name you enter. We never see or collect this information ourselves.

Permissions

The app needs internet access to download the schedule. It doesn't ask for or use any other device permission.

Changes to this policy

If anything here ever changes — for instance, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does send data somewhere — this page will be updated first, with the date above changed to reflect it.
